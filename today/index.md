# Today Screen
Rules and decisions governing the Today screen — its hero, food log, completion flag, and direct-manipulation gestures.

## Documents
* [2026-06-16 Design Handoff Revamp (ui_kits/ios_app/index.html)](design-handoff-2026-06-16.md) - The design-handoff revamp iterating the MUJI / dotted-globe app, overriding locked rules across the hero, composer, navigation, food log, and Trends.
* [Today Hero — Dotted Macro Globe + Legend](today-hero-globe.md) - The Today hero is a DottedGlobe beside a macro legend and Food-benefit row, replacing the retired water-washed strips.
* [Food Log Rendering — Today Page is Meal-Grouped (Plain)](food-log-rendering.md) - On Today the food log is plain (no card/glass) and grouped into Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner/Snacks sections with PlainDesignFoodRow rows.
* [Per-Day Completion Flag — DEFAULT OFF (inverted 2026-06-21)](per-day-completion.md) - Per-day completion is opt-in and default OFF; complete days get a full-color calorie bar and count in Trends, incomplete days show a dotted bar and are excluded.
* [Drag-to-Move Food Entries (long-press)](drag-to-move.md) - Long-pressing a Today food row drags it onto another meal section (change meal) or a trend day (change date).
