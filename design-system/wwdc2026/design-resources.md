# Apple Design Resources (iOS 27)

> **Canonical sources:**
> [Apple Design Resources](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/) (download hub) ·
> secondary: [9to5Mac — iOS 27 design resources released, June 11 2026](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/11/design-resources-for-ios-27-ipados-27-and-macos-27-golden-gate-now-available/).
> The kits/fonts are binary design files (Sketch/Figma/.dmg) — this page records *what
> exists and where*; download from the link to actually use them.

## UI kits
- **iOS 27 & iPadOS 27:** Sketch (Figma "coming soon" per 9to5Mac, as of mid-June 2026).
- **macOS 27:** Sketch. iOS/iPadOS/macOS **26:** Figma + Sketch. watchOS 26, visionOS 26: Figma + Sketch.
- Kits cover light + dark assets for buttons, headers, lists, input fields, alerts, and every system app icon.

## App icon templates
- iOS 27 / iPadOS 27: Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator, Sketch. **The icon shape/structure did not change from iOS 26.**

## System fonts
- SF Pro, SF Compact (small sizes / watchOS), SF Mono (code), New York (serif), plus SF Arabic/Armenian/Georgian/Hebrew.

## Tools
- **SF Symbols 8 beta** — 7,000+ symbols, 9 weights, 3 scales. (SF Symbols 7 stable.)
- **Icon Composer** — layered icons with Liquid Glass for iOS/iPadOS/macOS/watchOS.
- Parallax Previewer / Exporter (tvOS/visionOS).

## Technology templates (Figma/Sketch)
App Clips, Apple Pay, Live Activities, iMessage stickers, Sign in with Apple, Siri & App Shortcuts, Tap to Pay, TipKit, Wallet — plus product bezels (iPhone 17, etc.).

## Relevance to Nutritionist
- Design against the **iOS 27 Sketch UI Kit** and **SF Symbols 8**; use **Icon Composer** for the app icon (and as reference for the food-card Genmoji direction). Because the app-icon shape is unchanged from iOS 26, our icon needs no reshaping for iOS 27.
