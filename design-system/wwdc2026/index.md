# WWDC 2026 — Design

Distilled iOS-design guidance from WWDC 2026 (iOS 27) sessions. Each page pins its
**canonical Apple link** at the top — when the summary is thinner than the source, go to
the link (the session video / HIG page is authoritative).

## Documents
* [Principles of Great Design (WWDC26)](principles-of-great-design.md) - the eight foundational design principles for Apple-platform UI.
* [Communicate Your Brand Identity on iOS (WWDC26)](brand-identity-on-ios.md) - UI-layer vs content-layer model under Liquid Glass, plus color/typography/icon/logo guidance.
* [Design Intuitive Search Experiences (WWDC26)](design-search-experiences.md) - search-field component, per-platform placement, suggestions, filters/tokens, empty states.
